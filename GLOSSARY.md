# SMF Training Tracker

Tracks how many Trainings each Person attends per year and whether they reach the Training Requirement.

## Training Requirement

**Training Requirement**:
The minimum number of Trainings (26) a Person must attend within a year.
_Avoid_: Training goal, quota

**Reached**:
The status of a Person who attended at least as many Trainings as the Training Requirement demands within a year.
_Avoid_: Met, fulfilled, achieved

**Almost Reached**:
The status of a Person who attended 20–25 Trainings within a year. A Reached Person is never also Almost Reached.
_Avoid_: Almost met, nearly fulfilled

**Not Reached**:
The status of a Person who attended fewer than 20 Trainings within a year.
_Avoid_: Unmet, unreached, failed
